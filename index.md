I'm Peter, an Applied Scientist at Microsoft in Redmond, Washington and recent Ph.D. graduate from Brown University. I completed my doctorate in the [Department of Earth, Environmental, and Planetary Sciences (DEEPS)](https://deeps.brown.edu/) and the [Data Science Institute at Brown (DSI)](https://dsi.brown.edu/), working with [Dr. Karianne J. Bergen](https://www.kariannebergen.com/) in the [SciML Research Group](https://sites.brown.edu/bergen-lab/).

I'm broadly interested in using machine learning to make hard problems more tractable, across both scientific and applied domains. During my Ph.D., I built neural network emulators for climate and ice sheet modeling, developing tools that make computationally expensive simulations faster and more interpretable. That foundation in scientific ML now informs my work at Microsoft, where I build predictive models, optimization algorithms, and AI evaluation frameworks for Power Platform---translating research methods into systems used by millions of users.
